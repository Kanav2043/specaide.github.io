How we work

A simple, spec-first process that makes it easier for you to move from intent to procurement.

1

Share your requirement

Send a BOQ, drawing set, or tell us what stage your project is at - we’ll clarify scope quickly.

2

Compare options

We propose solutions across brands, budgets, lead-times and performance - including value engineering.

3

Samples & submittals

We support the specification pack: samples, technical sheets, approvals and procurement-ready documentation.

4

Supply & installation

We coordinate delivery and, where required, installation to ensure finish quality and timely handover.

5

After-sales support

Dealer-backed support and service for issues, replacements and ongoing requirements.
